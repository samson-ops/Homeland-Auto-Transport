Before Pickup Day

Preparing Your Car For Shipment

A little preparation goes a long way. Follow these simple steps for a smooth, hassle-free shipping experience.

01

Clean Your Vehicle

Wash your car inside and out so an accurate condition inspection can be completed before shipping.

02

Check Fluid Levels

Ensure oil, coolant, brake fluid, and power steering fluid are all at adequate levels.

03

Check the Battery

Make sure your battery is fully charged and in good working condition for loading and unloading.

04

Inspect Tires

Check tire pressure and condition — proper inflation helps prevent delays during transport.

05

Remove Personal Items

Take out all personal belongings. Carriers are not responsible for items left inside the vehicle.

06

Disable Alarms

Turn off any alarm systems to prevent disruptions while your vehicle is loaded and in transit.

What To Expect

Our Car Shipping Process

01

We Secure Your Carrier

We post your vehicle across our network, including the national dispatch boards for auto transport, and present offers from FMCSA-licensed, insured carriers. When you accept, we send an agreement that guarantees your price, door-to-door service, and 100% bumper-to-bumper insurance.

02

Your Vehicle Is Picked Up

Once you approve the carrier and final logistics, you pay a deposit that's included in — not added to — your quoted total. The remaining balance is due at delivery, and nothing moves forward without your approval.

03

The Carrier Delivers Your Vehicle

Your driver contacts you before pickup and delivery to schedule convenient times. At the destination, you complete a final inspection, pay the balance, and both you and the driver sign the Bill of Lading — each keeping a copy.
