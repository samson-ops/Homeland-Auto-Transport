Before Pickup Day

Preparing Your Car For Auto Transport

A little preparation goes a long way. Follow these simple steps for a smooth, hassle-free car shipping experience.

01

Clean Your Vehicle

Wash your car inside and out so an accurate condition inspection can be completed before auto transport.

02

Check Fluid Levels

Ensure oil, coolant, brake fluid, and power steering fluid are all at adequate levels.

03

Check the Battery

Make sure your battery is fully charged and in good working condition for loading and unloading.

04

Inspect Tires

Check tire pressure and condition — proper inflation helps prevent delays during transport.

05

Remove Personal Items

Take out all personal belongings. Carriers are not responsible for items left inside the vehicle.

06

Disable Alarms

Turn off any alarm systems to prevent disruptions while your car is loaded and shipped.

Our Auto Transport Process

01

We Secure Your Carrier

We post your vehicle across our nationwide auto transport network, including the national dispatch boards, and present offers from FMCSA-licensed, insured car shipping carriers. When you accept, we send an agreement that guarantees your price, door-to-door service, and 100% bumper-to-bumper insurance.

02

Your Vehicle Is Picked Up

Once you approve the carrier and final logistics, you pay a deposit that's included in — not added to — your quoted total. The remaining balance is due at delivery, and nothing moves forward without your approval.

03

The Carrier Delivers Your Vehicle

Your auto transport driver contacts you before pickup and delivery to schedule convenient times. At the destination, you complete a final inspection, pay the balance, and both you and the driver sign the Bill of Lading — each keeping a copy.

Auto transport time is driven mainly by distance. Shorter regional car shipping moves often take 1–3 days, cross-country shipments typically take 7–10 days, and coast-to-coast routes can run a little longer. Your driver contacts you ahead of pickup and delivery so you always have an estimated window.
