The city now tops 900,000 residents and anchors the western half of a Metroplex of eight million, which makes Dallas–Fort Worth one of the three or four busiest auto transport markets in the country. If you're shipping a car to or from Fort Worth, you're operating in a market where carriers compete hard for your load, and that works entirely in your favor.

What Actually Moves The Price

Route density is Fort Worth's advantage: the I-35W and I-20 corridors carry so much transport traffic that per-mile rates here run below the national average on most lanes. Vehicle size is the next factor — this is truck country, and a crew-cab dually or a Yukon XL takes meaningfully more deck space and weight than a sedan, typically adding $150–$300 on the same route. Season matters twice a year: summer relocation season tightens long-haul capacity, and spring hail events (more on that below) can spike short-term demand for enclosed trailers across the entire Metroplex within hours. Transport type and condition round it out — enclosed adds 30–50%, and a non-runner needs a winch-equipped carrier for a modest additional fee.

Comparing Fort Worth and Dallas quotes? They're effectively the same market to a carrier — most quotes are identical within a few dollars either side of the Metroplex. What actually changes your price is pickup accessibility. A downtown high-rise on Throckmorton is a harder stop for a 75-foot rig than a driveway in Keller, so flexible meeting spots sometimes shave real money off a quote.

Open vs. Enclosed Auto Transport In Fort Worth

Open car transport handles roughly nine of every ten vehicles nationwide and is the right call for most Fort Worth shipments — it's the same two-level equipment that restocks the Alliance-corridor dealerships weekly, and cargo insurance covers the vehicle bumper to bumper for the whole trip.

Enclosed auto transport deserves a harder look in North Texas than in most of the country, for one reason: hail. The DFW Metroplex sits squarely in one of the most hail-prone regions in America, and spring supercells have produced some of the costliest hail losses in U.S. history within an hour's drive of downtown. A car on an open trailer during a surprise April storm has the same exposure as a car in a parking lot. For high-value vehicles, fresh paint or wraps, classics headed to or from the area's big collector events, and any shipment scheduled between March and May, the 30–50% enclosed premium is often the cheapest insurance you can buy. The rest of the year, open transport is usually the sensible economic choice.

Military & Corporate Relocations Into The Metroplex

Fort Worth handles two steady streams of relocation shipping that most cities don't. The first is military. Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base Fort Worth hosts Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, and Texas Air National Guard units, and every PCS cycle brings service members who need a POV moved on a government timeline. We work with report dates in mind, provide the documentation your transportation office expects, and can deliver to base-adjacent addresses on the west side with drivers who know which gates a transporter can and cannot approach.

The second stream is corporate. The Metroplex has drawn a run of major headquarters and campus relocations over the past decade, and each one moves employees — and their vehicles — from California, Illinois, and the Northeast into Tarrant County suburbs like Southlake, Keller, and Mansfield. If your employer offers a relocation benefit, vehicle shipping is usually reimbursable; we provide itemized, all-inclusive invoices formatted for relocation-package submission, and we coordinate pickup at your origin around your household-goods movers so the car arrives when you do, not a week after.

How Our Fort Worth Car Shipping Process Works

The process is the same for a commuter sedan out of Arlington or a show truck out of Aledo, and nothing moves without your approval. Step one, the quote — instant on this page or by phone, all-inclusive with door-to-door service, fuel, and full insurance. Step two, carrier assignment. Preparing Your Car For Auto Transport In Fort Worth

Pickup goes fastest when the vehicle is ready: washed for accurate inspection photos, personal items and toll tags removed (unmount your TollTag so it doesn't ping the North Tarrant Express gantries from the trailer), about a quarter tank of fuel, alarm off, battery charged, tires inflated.

Two local notes from our dispatchers. First, if you're shipping between March and May, ask us about the hail outlook when we schedule — moving a pickup by a single day around a forecast supercell is routine for us and can spare you an insurance claim. Second, much of the newer growth in Tarrant and Parker counties sits behind gated entries or on streets a full-size transporter can't legally turn around in; flag it at booking and your driver will set a meeting point at a nearby big-box lot rather than improvising on the day.
